# Sun step with the shadow cache: right after a step the current set's cascades k > 0 are re-rendered
# one per frame; until then they are marked stale (stats.render.shadow_stale, bit k) and the shader takes
# the previous set for their pixels (no lighter, grainy shadow for 1-3 frames).
# Pixel check (shadow term stays flat during the cross-fade) was done with an 8-degree step at 0.5 fps.
#   node tools/bridge.mjs run games/demo/tests/sunstep.txt
reload
screen play
wait 1000
pause
call render {"shadow_step_deg": 8, "shadow_fade_ms": 5000, "shadow_cascades": 4}
call sky {"time": 14.0, "day_length": 0}
frames 10
call sky {"time": 14.6}
frames 10
call sky {"time": 15.2}
frames 1
expect stats.render.shadow_fade > 0.5
expect stats.render.shadow_stale > 0
frames 5
expect stats.render.shadow_stale == 0
expect logs{"level": "error"}.entries.length == 0
